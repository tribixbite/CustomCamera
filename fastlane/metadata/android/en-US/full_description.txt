CustomCamera is a fast, private camera with real manual controls — no ads, no account, and no network access at all.

<b>Pro mode with real sensor control</b>
• Set ISO, shutter speed, white balance and manual focus directly on the camera sensor
• Live readouts of the automatic exposure while you shoot
• Save RAW (DNG) next to every JPEG on supported cameras
• Focus peaking highlights what is sharp while you focus by hand
• Live histogram with highlight and shadow clipping warnings

<b>Every lens, every mode</b>
• Photo, Video, Pro and QR Scan modes, plus Night and Portrait where your phone supports them
• One-tap zoom presets (0.6×, 1×, 2×, 3×, 5× depending on your phone) and pinch to zoom
• Choose any camera your phone exposes, with automatic fallback if one fails
• Ultra HDR photos and 10-bit HDR video on supported devices

<b>Video</b>
• Up to 4K, with stabilization and pause / resume
• Switch between front and back cameras while recording
• Take photos while recording
• Dual video: record the front and back cameras at once, picture-in-picture, on supported phones
• Adjustable torch brightness on supported phones

<b>Made to be quick</b>
• Tap to focus, long-press to lock focus and exposure, slide to adjust brightness
• Swipe sideways to change mode, swipe up or down to flip the camera
• Self-timer, composition grids, level indicator and volume-key shutter
• Screen flash for selfies
• Tablet and foldable layout: on large screens the controls move to a side rail in landscape

<b>Crop and rotate</b>
• Crop, rotate and mirror photos in the built-in viewer; edits are saved as a new copy and the original is never changed

<b>QR and barcode scanner</b>
• Open links, join Wi-Fi networks, call, email, text or view locations straight from a code
• Scanning runs entirely on your phone

<b>Private by design</b>
• Photos and videos are saved to your gallery under DCIM/CustomCamera
• Location tagging is off unless you turn it on, and is only stored inside your files
• The app cannot access the internet and collects no data

Works with other apps: CustomCamera can take photos and videos when another app asks for the camera.

Some features depend on what your phone's camera hardware and Android version support; unsupported options are hidden automatically.
